Privacy

Short version: Walkki doesn't collect anything.

What Walkki reads

With your permission, Walkki reads your step count from Apple Health on your iPhone and Apple Watch. It only reads — it never writes to Apple Health.

What Walkki stores

Your daily goal, your reminder settings, and today's step count (so the widget can show it) are saved on your device only. Your goal is also sent to your paired Apple Watch so the watch ring can use it.

What Walkki shares

Nothing. Walkki has no account, no server, no analytics, and no advertising. Your data is never uploaded, sold, or shared with anyone, including Theprahok Inc., the company that makes Walkki.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No data is sent anywhere to deliver them.

Deleting your data

Deleting Walkki removes everything it stored. Your step history stays in Apple Health, which Walkki doesn't own or change.
